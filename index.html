Work Process

Every great digital experience starts with understanding people. I follow a human-centered Design Thinking process to transform ideas into meaningful, intuitive, and effective digital experiences.

Throughout the design process, I collaborate closely with stakeholders, product owners, and developers to align user needs, communicate design progress, and continuously refine solutions through feedback, ensuring a seamless and impactful product experience.

1. Research & Define

Understanding users through research, interviews, and observations, then translating insights into clear problem statements and design priorities.

2. Ideate

Exploring potential solutions through brainstorming, user flows, and information architecture to identify the most effective approach.

3. Design & Prototype

Transforming ideas into wireframes, interactive prototypes, and polished interfaces, refining the experience through feedback and usability testing.

4. Test & Deliver

Validating design solutions, preparing developer handoff, and continuously improving the product based on user feedback.
